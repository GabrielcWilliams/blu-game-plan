        Where each class stands

        House rules

        1. Sunday deadlines are Saturday deadlines. Anything due Sunday or Monday is submitted by Saturday at 2pm.
        2. Never turn anything in late. Thermo gives a zero. Strength takes 25% off and Dynamics 20% off, and neither accepts work more than 24 hours late.
        3. Never miss Thermo. Quizzes are unannounced and there are no makeups.
        4. 6 to 8am is quiet time. Homework stays out of the mornings.
        5. A missed block moves, it does not disappear. Make it up in the next open slot that week.
        6. Normal weeks build the buffer. Open slots go to next week's homework, so exam weeks are free for exam prep.
        7. Protect sleep before exams. In bed by midnight the night before any exam.
        8. Check every rubric. Strength and Dynamics reject handwritten work in pen, on plain paper, without a title page, or with the wrong file name.
        9. Do both course evaluations. Dynamics and Strength each add up to 2%.

        Exam prep

        Prep starts 10 to 14 days out, and Saturday 9am to 2pm always goes to the next exam. The rule for every calculation class: solve from a blank page, peek at one step only when stuck, then finish alone. The point is learning how to think through the problem, not copying a solution.
